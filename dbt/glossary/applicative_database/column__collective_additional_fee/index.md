**collective_additional_fee_id**: Identifier of the additional fee applied to a collective stock.

**collective_additional_fee_type**: Type of the additional fee associated with the collective stock (ACCOMMODATION, APPLICATION_FEE, BROADCASTING, CONSUMABLE_ITEMS, COPYRIGHT, MANAGEMENT_FEE, MEAL, OTHER, TRAVEL).

**collective_additional_fee_label**: Label describing the additional fee.

**collective_additional_fee_amount**: Amount charged for the additional fee.

**total_additional_fee_amount**: Sum of the additional fee amounts declared on the collective stock.

**total_additional_fee_types**: Number of distinct additional fee types declared on the collective stock.

**collective_stock_has_additional_fee**: Whether the collective stock has at least one additional fee declared.
