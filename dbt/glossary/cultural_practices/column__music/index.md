# Cultural Practices 2018 — Music columns

**music_genre**: Music genre.

**listens_to_genre**: 1 if the respondent listens to this genre, else 0.

**likes_genre**: 1 if the respondent particularly likes this genre, else 0.

**dislikes_genre**: 1 if the respondent does not like this genre at all, else 0.

**music_listening_frequency_code**: Raw survey code for music listening frequency.

**music_support_cd_cassette**: 1 if the respondent listens to music on CD or cassette, else 0.

**music_support_vinyl**: 1 if the respondent listens to music on vinyl, else 0.

**music_support_streaming_specialized**: 1 if the respondent listens to music via a specialized streaming service, else 0.

**music_support_streaming_other**: 1 if the respondent listens to music via another streaming service, else 0.

**music_support_digital_files**: 1 if the respondent listens to music via digital files, else 0.

**music_support_radio**: 1 if the respondent listens to music via the radio, else 0.

**music_support_tv**: 1 if the respondent listens to music via the television, else 0.

**music_at_home_frequency_code**: Raw survey code for how often the respondent listens to music at home.

**music_active_listening_code**: Raw survey code for how often the respondent actively listens to music.

**music_importance_code**: Raw survey code for the importance of music to the respondent.

**music_listening_frequency**: How often the respondent listens to music.

**music_foreign_language**: Whether the respondent listens to music in a foreign language.

**music_at_home_frequency**: How often the respondent listens to music at home.

**music_active_listening**: How often the respondent actively listens to music.

**music_importance**: How important music is to the respondent.
