## Collective (EAC)

Collective part of the pass Culture (EAC): school and student participation, credit consumption, collective bookings and offers.

**Data protection**: Not perturbed: ADAGE aggregates of school headcounts, institutions (public bodies), bookings and offers. Bookings by school geography are not secret (public money); bookings by cultural partner geography are under the **statistical secret** of external reporting. See [Statistical confidentiality](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/references/statistical_confidentiality/index.md).
