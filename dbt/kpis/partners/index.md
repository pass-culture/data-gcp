## Cultural partners

Active and activated cultural partners, and their bookings and revenue from individual bookings by venue territory.

**Data protection**: Partner counts are neither perturbed nor secret (lists of partners are public). Bookings and revenue by venue territory are under the **statistical secret** of external reporting: a secret department is hidden on every month. No category breakdown on the partner side: categories are read on the young people's side ([Usage](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/kpis/usage/index.md)). See [Statistical confidentiality](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/references/statistical_confidentiality/index.md).
