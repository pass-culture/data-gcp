The `external_reporting__eac_bookings_count` model counts, for each territory (school geography), the collective bookings of its schools over the last 12 complete months, for every school side KPI of the collective reporting: amounts and bookings (`*_etablissements`), domain and format breakdowns, credit usage. `external_reporting__eac` masks these KPIs, on every month, when 1 to 4 bookings account for them, since they would disclose single transactions. Counts of schools stay visible.
