The `external_reporting__beneficiary_booking_bookers` model counts, for each territory (user geography) and beneficiary booking KPI, the distinct beneficiaries who made a used booking from January 2022 to the last complete month. `external_reporting__individual` masks the KPIs that 1 to 4 beneficiaries account for, on every month, since amounts and booking counts cannot be blurred. The distinct bookers are the new bookers of [`metrics_booking__individual_bookers`](#!/model/model.data_gcp_dbt.metrics_booking__individual_bookers) summed from January 2022 to the last complete month: new bookers add up across territories, distinct bookers do not.
