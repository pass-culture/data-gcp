## VIDOC exports

VIDOC is the Ministry of Culture dashboard. pass Culture feeds it with the `exp_vidoc__*` tables (BigQuery dataset `export_vidoc_{env}`). The lot 1 tables are also sent to the ministry S3 bucket by the `export_vidoc` DAG, which only sends the columns validated by VIDOC (lot 1, then lot 2.1).

The KPIs computed from these tables are defined in [KPIs](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/kpis/index.md).

### Lot 2.1: diversity and credit use at expiration

The first lot 2 indicators are sent in the `diversity` table. VIDOC validated them in dev and staging, so prod sends them too. Dev and staging get all the columns of the exported tables, prod only the validated ones (`VIDOC_MODELS` in the `export_vidoc` DAG).

| Sheet | Indicator                                                          | Columns of `diversity`                                                                                                           | Definition                                                                                                                  |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FI2.3 | Average number of distinct venues booked at expiration             | `total_distinct_venue_booked_at_expiration`, `total_expired_deposit_beneficiaries`                                               | [Distinct venues booked](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/kpis/diversity/distinct_venues_booked/index.md)        |
| FI2.4 | Share of young people who booked in at least N categories (1 to 5) | `total_1plus_category_booked_beneficiaries` … `total_5plus_category_booked_beneficiaries`, `total_expired_deposit_beneficiaries` | [Diversity depth](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/kpis/diversity/diversity_category_depth/index.md)             |
| FI3.1 | Young people by share of credit spent                              | `total_spent_share_0_beneficiaries`, then `_0_10` … `_80_90`, `_90_100` (10-point steps)                                         | [Spent share distribution](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/kpis/usage/credit_spent_share_distribution/index.md) |
| FI3.2 | Average amount granted, spent, and spending rate                   | `total_deposit_amount_at_expiration`, `total_actual_amount_spent_at_expiration`, `total_expired_deposit_beneficiaries`           | [Credit amounts at expiration](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/kpis/usage/credit_amount_at_expiration/index.md) |

Counts of young people are perturbed (cell key method) and must be summed before dividing. Amounts and the number of venues are not perturbed: they are empty on rows with fewer than 5 young people before perturbation, so averages must leave those rows out of the numerator **and** the denominator.

### Open data

The lot 1 beneficiary and diversity tables are also published as open data on [data.gouv.fr](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/data_gouv/index.md). Both exports read the same perturbed base (`exp_base__*`), so the open data figures are the VIDOC figures. The data.gouv.fr resources keep only the lot 1 columns and closed months, and format geographic codes for data.gouv.fr.

### Data protection

Beneficiary counts are perturbed with the cell key method; partner and finance figures follow the external reporting rules. See [Statistical confidentiality](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/references/statistical_confidentiality/index.md).
