## Vidoc KPIs

This section documents the key performance indicators (KPIs) exported for the ministry via the **vidoc** export tables. For each indicator you will find the definition, source table(s), SQL calculation rule, and available dimensions.

All vidoc export tables are documented individually in the **Table definitions** section under `export > vidoc`.

### Data protection

All exported counts are protected using the **Cell Key Perturbation method** — the current standard recommended by INSEE and Eurostat. Small counts are always slightly modified so that no individual can be re-identified, while large counts and aggregated totals remain virtually exact. See [Statistical confidentiality](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/references/statistical_confidentiality/index.md) for full details.

The EAC (collective part) tables are the exception: `exp_vidoc_student_coverage` holds ADAGE aggregates of school headcounts and `exp_vidoc_institution_activity` counts institutions (public bodies) and their credit, so neither is perturbed. The booking exports follow the external reporting rules instead. Cultural partner revenue (`exp_vidoc_booking_partner_finance_individual`) is protected by **statistical secret**: departments secret at the last complete month (fewer than 3 partners with revenue, one partner above 85% of the revenue, or secondary secret) are hidden on every month; it has no category breakdown. Categories are read on the young people's side (practices), as in the observatoire: bookings and spending of young people by territory of residence (`exp_vidoc_booking_beneficiary`) are hidden on every month when fewer than 5 beneficiaries account for them (20 for the category breakdown).
