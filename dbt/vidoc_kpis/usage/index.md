## Usage

Use of the individual credit by young people: amounts granted and spent at expiration, and bookings by territory of residence and category.

**Data protection**: Counts are perturbed (cell key method); amounts at expiration are hidden when fewer than 5 beneficiaries are in the row. Bookings by territory of residence are not perturbed but hidden on every month when fewer than 5 beneficiaries account for them (20 for the category breakdown), as in external reporting. See [Statistical confidentiality](https://pass-culture.github.io/data-gcp/dbt/references/statistical_confidentiality/index.md).
