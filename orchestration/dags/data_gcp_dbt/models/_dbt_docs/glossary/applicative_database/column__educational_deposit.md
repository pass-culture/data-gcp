{% docs column__educational_deposit_period %} Part of the scholar year the deposit covers: all_year (September to August), sept-dec or jan-aug. Before the scholar year was split into two allocations, every institution received a single all_year deposit; since then most institutions receive a sept-dec and a jan-aug deposit, while some still receive all_year, depending on the year and the institution's ministry. The period describes how the money was released, not a filter for a scholar-year amount: that amount sums every period of the scholar year. {% enddocs %}
