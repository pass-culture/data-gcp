{% docs column__has_received_recredit_17 %} Whether the deposit received a RECREDIT_17 (the age-17 credit: 50€ on a post-reform GRANT_17_18 deposit; 30€ on a pre-reform GRANT_15_17 deposit). To count young people who received the 17 credit, use this flag with `deposit_type = 'GRANT_17_18'`, not `deposit_reform_category = '17_post_reform'`, which only reflects the last stage of the deposit and misses those since recredited at 18. Former GRANT_15_17 beneficiaries (born up to February 2010) receive their GRANT_17_18 deposit and its RECREDIT_17 automatically on their 17th birthday, with no action on their part: for those generations the 17-credit rate mostly reflects the former 15-17 scheme, not a sign-up — separate them from the young people who signed up for it. {% enddocs %}

{% docs column__has_received_recredit_18 %} Whether the deposit received a RECREDIT_18 (the age-18 credit, 150€ on a GRANT_17_18 deposit, also recorded when the deposit is created directly at 18). To count young people who received the 18 credit, use this flag with `deposit_type = 'GRANT_17_18'` rather than `deposit_reform_category`. {% enddocs %}
{% docs column__first_recredit_18_date %} The date of the first RECREDIT_18 (age-18 credit) on the deposit; null if none. It can fall after the beneficiary's 19th birthday (late identity validations): to compare generations, bound it at the 19th birthday. {% enddocs %}
