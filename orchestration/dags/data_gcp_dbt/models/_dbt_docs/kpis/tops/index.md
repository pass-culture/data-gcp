## Tops

Monthly top 50 offers and cultural venues by used booking revenue, at national, regional and departmental levels.

**Data protection**: Only the first 50 entities of each ranking are exported; amounts are published per offer and per venue, as in the external reporting tops. Not part of the vidoc lot 1 export to the ministry. See [Statistical confidentiality](../../references/statistical_confidentiality.md).

| KPI | Definition | VIDOC table(s) | data.gouv.fr | Tracking |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| [Top offers](top_offer_ranking.md) | Top 50 items by used booking revenue | `exp_vidoc_top_offer` | – | Monthly |
| [Top offers by category](top_offer_category_ranking.md) | Top 50 items by used booking revenue within each offer category | `exp_vidoc_top_offer_category` | – | Monthly |
| [Top offers in labelled venues](top_offer_labelled_ranking.md) | Top 50 items by used booking revenue in venues with a Ministry of Culture label | `exp_vidoc_top_offer_labelled` | – | Monthly |
| [Top venues](top_venue_ranking.md) | Top 50 venues by used booking revenue | `exp_vidoc_top_venue` | – | Monthly |
| [Top labelled venues](top_venue_labelled_ranking.md) | Top 50 venues with a Ministry of Culture label by used booking revenue | `exp_vidoc_top_venue_labelled` | – | Monthly |
