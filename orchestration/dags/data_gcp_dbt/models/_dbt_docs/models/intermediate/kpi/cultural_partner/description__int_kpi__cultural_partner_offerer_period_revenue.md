{% docs description__int_kpi__cultural_partner_offerer_period_revenue %}
Revenue per offerer and municipality over fixed periods of `statistical_secret_period_months` months starting on `partition_month`: calendar years for individual bookings, school years for collective bookings (completed periods only). Only positive revenues are kept: they define the contributors of the statistical secret rule.
