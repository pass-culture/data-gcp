## Departmental Financial and Booking Metrics

### Definition

Aggregated operational and financial metrics generated by individual bookings, consolidated at the venue's geographic level (department and region) for a given partition month.

The metrics include:
- **Total bookings**: Number of booking transactions.
- **Total quantities**: Total volume of items or tickets booked.
- **Total revenue amount**: Gross transaction value generated by bookings.
- **Total reimbursed amount**: Total amount reimbursed to cultural partners.
- **Total contribution amount**: Total contribution or co-financing amount.

### Source table

[exp_vidoc__booking_finance_individual](../models/export/vidoc/description__exp_vidoc__booking_finance_individual.md)

### Calculation

Sum the operational and financial metric columns over the desired dimensions.

```sql
SELECT
    partition_month,
    venue_region_name,
    venue_department_code,
    venue_department_name,
    SUM(total_bookings) AS total_bookings,
    SUM(total_quantities) AS total_quantities,
    SUM(total_revenue_amount) AS total_revenue_amount,
    SUM(total_reimbursed_amount) AS total_reimbursed_amount,
    SUM(total_contribution_amount) AS total_contribution_amount
FROM `exp_vidoc__booking_finance_individual`
GROUP BY
    partition_month,
    venue_region_name,
    venue_department_code,
    venue_department_name
ORDER BY
    partition_month,
    venue_department_code
